About us

Bakossi Cultural and Development Association of Calgary

A non-profit bringing together people of Bakossi origin, their families and friends across Calgary and surrounding communities in Alberta.

Who we are

Preserving a legacy, building connection

The Bakossi Cultural and Development Association of Calgary, Canada (BACDA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to bringing together people of Bakossi origin, their families, and friends across the city of Calgary and surrounding communities in the province of Alberta, Canada.

BACDA provides a platform for cultural preservation, community development, mutual support and social engagement. The organization is committed to preserving Bakossi traditions, promoting cultural awareness among younger generations and strengthening relationships within the Bakossi community and the wider Canadian society.

BACDA Calgary is the Calgary chapter of the association, serving as a local platform for members and friends to connect, celebrate their heritage, support one another and contribute positively to the Calgary community.

We believe that our cultural identity is a valuable legacy that should be preserved, celebrated and passed on to future generations.

Our history

Since 2013

BACDA Calgary was established in 2013 with a shared vision of uniting people of Bakossi origin from Cameroon and their friends across Canada through cultural identity, community solidarity and collective development.

Recognizing the importance of preserving our heritage while building meaningful connections in Canadian society, the association provides a common platform for members to celebrate their roots, support one another and work towards shared community goals.

Through volunteer participation, cultural activities, community projects and social initiatives, BACDA continues to encourage unity and strengthen the bonds connecting Bakossi families and friends.

BACDA Calgary represents this shared vision locally, creating opportunities for cultural expression, newcomer support, youth engagement and community participation within Calgary and surrounding areas.

Our history is a continuing story of identity, belonging, service and togetherness.

Mission, vision and values

What guides us

To preserve and promote Bakossi cultural heritage, strengthen community bonds, support development initiatives and encourage mutual support, cultural awareness and integration within Canadian society.

Our vision

To build a united, vibrant and empowered Bakossi community across Canada that proudly preserves its cultural identity, supports its members and contributes meaningfully to Canadian society.

Our core values

  • Unity and solidarity
  • Cultural preservation and pride
  • Mutual respect and inclusion
  • Volunteerism and service
  • Education and empowerment
  • Community development
Our commitment to Calgary

Heritage and integration go hand in hand

BACDA Calgary is committed to being an active and positive contributor to Calgary's multicultural community.

Through cultural festivals, educational workshops, newcomer mentorship, youth empowerment, family support and community outreach, we seek to create an environment where people feel welcomed, connected and empowered.

We recognize that preserving our heritage and integrating into Canadian society go hand in hand. Our goal is to celebrate our identity while building meaningful relationships with the wider community.

Through all our activities, we strive to foster unity, cultural pride, mutual support and active community participation, strengthening ties within the Cameroonian diaspora and with Calgary's wider multicultural community.

Our goal is simple: to build a connected, empowered and culturally vibrant community where everyone has an opportunity to belong, contribute and thrive.

Programs and services

Preserving heritage, creating opportunity

Our programs are designed to preserve Bakossi cultural heritage, strengthen community relationships and create opportunities for personal and collective growth.

Through volunteer involvement, cultural activities, educational initiatives and community support, we bring Bakossi families and friends together while encouraging meaningful participation in Canadian society.

Program 1

Cultural preservation and promotion

We celebrate and promote Bakossi traditions, values and cultural identity through:

  • Cultural festivals and traditional celebrations
  • Traditional music, dance and cultural performances
  • Bakossi language preservation and learning initiatives
  • Cultural education and knowledge sharing between generations
  • Activities that encourage younger generations to appreciate their heritage
Program 2

Newcomer integration and family support

We encourage a welcoming and supportive environment for Bakossi newcomers and families settling in Calgary and surrounding communities.

  • Connecting newcomers with established community members
  • Mentorship, networking and information sharing
  • Guidance and informal support during settlement
  • Encouraging family connections and community participation
  • Sharing useful information about available community resources
Program 3

Youth empowerment and development

Young people are essential to the future of our community. We encourage youth participation, leadership and personal development through:

  • Youth engagement and mentorship opportunities
  • Leadership development and volunteer participation
  • Cultural education and intergenerational learning
  • Encouraging academic achievement and personal growth
  • Opportunities for young people to contribute their ideas and talents
Program 4

Education and community awareness

We promote learning, cultural understanding and awareness within our community and the wider Canadian society through:

  • Educational and cultural awareness activities
  • Knowledge sharing and community discussions
  • Promoting understanding of Bakossi history and traditions
  • Encouraging respect for cultural diversity and inclusion
  • Supporting opportunities for intergenerational learning
Program 5

Community outreach and social engagement

We encourage members to contribute positively to Calgary and the communities in which we live through:

  • Volunteer-driven community initiatives
  • Social gatherings and networking activities
  • Community outreach and charitable participation
  • Collaboration with other cultural and community organizations
  • Initiatives that promote unity, cooperation and mutual support
Program 6

Community events and networking

We provide opportunities for members, families and friends to connect, celebrate and build lasting relationships through:

  • Monthly general meetings and community gatherings
  • Cultural celebrations and special events
  • Family and social activities
  • Community networking and partnership opportunities
  • Activities that strengthen ties within the Bakossi and wider Cameroonian communities

Membership

Be part of our community, preserve our heritage, build our future

BACDA Calgary welcomes individuals and families who share our commitment to preserving Bakossi cultural heritage, promoting unity and strengthening our community.

Membership is an opportunity to connect with fellow Bakossi people, celebrate our cultural identity, take part in community activities and contribute to initiatives that support our collective growth.

Whether you are a long-standing member of the community, a newcomer to Calgary or a friend who appreciates Bakossi culture, there is a place for you here.

Who can become a member?

  • Individuals of Bakossi origin and their descendants
  • Spouses and family members of Bakossi people
  • Friends and supporters who share the association's vision and values
  • Anyone interested in promoting Bakossi culture, unity and community development

Member responsibilities

A shared responsibility

Membership comes with a shared responsibility to uphold the values and objectives of BACDA Calgary. Members are encouraged to:

  • Respect the association's bylaws, policies and guidelines
  • Promote unity, respect and cooperation among members
  • Take an active part in our monthly meetings and community activities
  • Support cultural preservation and community development initiatives
  • Treat fellow members with dignity and respect
  • Fulfil membership obligations, including approved dues and contributions
